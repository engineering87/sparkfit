# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want sparkfit to be a welcoming, friendly, and respectful project for everyone
who takes part, regardless of background or experience level. Maintainers and
contributors commit to keeping participation a positive experience for all.

## Our standards

We expect everyone in the community to:

- Be respectful and considerate in language and actions.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and give constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community.
- Show empathy toward other community members.

Conduct that makes others feel unwelcome is not acceptable, including personal
attacks and any behavior a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions, and code review) and when an individual represents the project in
public spaces.

## Standard reference

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Please refer to it for the full set of guidelines and enforcement principles.

## Reporting and enforcement

To report an issue, please contact the maintainer privately by email at
francesco.delre@protonmail.com. Reports will be reviewed and handled with
discretion. Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, including
warnings or removal from project spaces.
